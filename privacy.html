Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes how Kitefish ("we", "us") handles information in the games and apps we publish and on this website.

Information we collect

Unless stated otherwise in a specific game's store listing, our games do not require an account and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location.

Some games may use third-party services (for example the platform's app store, crash reporting, analytics or advertising). Those services may collect device identifiers and usage data according to their own privacy policies. Where a game uses such services, it will be disclosed in that game's store listing.

How we use information

Any data we receive is used only to operate, maintain and improve our games, fix bugs and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Contacting us by email

If you email us, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of any data associated with you by emailing info@kitefish.dev.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
