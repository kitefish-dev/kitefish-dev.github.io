Studio

Small studio, big on polish

We keep the team small and the scope focused, so every game gets the care players notice in the first few seconds.

  • Made for everyone

    Clear visuals, readable text and simple controls, so anyone can pick up our games and play, from first-time players to seasoned gamers.

  • Fair free-to-play

    Our games are free to play. Ads and purchases stay respectful: no pay-to-win, no tricks and no surprise charges.

  • Polished feel

    Every tap, drag and animation is tuned until it feels just right.

  • Light on your phone

    Small downloads that run smoothly on older phones and go easy on your battery.

  • Privacy first

    We collect as little data as possible and explain clearly what each game uses and why.
